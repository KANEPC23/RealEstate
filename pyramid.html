Letter of Transmittal

Proposal #11082

December 20, 2024 · Attn: Mr. Posner · YS 220W42 Holdings LLC c/o Yellowstone Real Estate

Pyramid Restoration NY LLC is pleased to submit our proposal to perform exterior repairs at 220 West 42nd Street. We shall supply all materials, permits, equipment and skilled mechanics.

Our price is built line by line off the SPAR Architects bid sheet. Every terracotta unit, cast stone and GFRC replica, steel repair and waterproofing assembly is carried at a stated unit rate, so quantities that move in the field settle against numbers the design team has already reviewed. All unit quantities are allowances: the architect confirms work locations after each area is mobilized, we maintain as-built drawings and a running quantity log submitted with each payment requisition, and unused allowance value is credited back to ownership except where material is retained as attic stock.

The sequence takes the building one elevation at a time — north, east, south, then west — with pipe and suspended scaffold relocated rather than duplicated. That holds equipment cost down and limits the footprint on 42nd Street to a single active face at any time.

Landmark-grade terracotta is the core of this scope and it is the work we do most. Cast stone and GFRC replicas are shop-glazed to match existing, mock-ups are set for architect review before production, anchorage is submitted under PE-sealed shop drawings, and 10% of post-installed anchors are pull-tested. No grinders are used on historic masonry.

Project Information

Per SPAR Architects sheet G000.00

The Candler Building is a skyscraper at the southern end of Times Square in the Midtown Manhattan neighborhood of New York City. The 24-story building was designed by the firm of Willauer, Shape and Bready in the Spanish Renaissance style and was constructed between 1912 and 1913 for Coca-Cola Company owner Asa Griggs Candler.

The proposed scope of work is for general construction at the facade only. No interior work or change in occupancy or egress is proposed as part of this application, and there are no related applications filed under this scope.

Project allowance conditions

  1. All unit quantities are to be considered allowances unless otherwise stated. The architect reviews and confirms work locations following mobilization of the designated work areas.
  2. The contractor maintains working as-built drawings documenting the location of performed work and distinguishing where additional work was performed beyond the unit quantities approved by the architect. These are submitted with each payment requisition.
  3. The contractor maintains a working log of all assigned and performed unit quantities for each work-type category, submitted with each payment requisition for architect review.
  4. Unused materials are returned to the vendor from which they were obtained and credited back to ownership per Section 012100 of the project manual, except where ownership has specifically requested the material be stored as attic stock.
  5. The contractor credits the value of all unused allowances except for the value of materials where ownership has requested purchase as attic stock.

Regulatory framework carried in this bid

Sidewalk sheds — BC 3307.6 / LL 43-85

  • Shed required prior to work above 40 feet; remains until facade work is complete
  • Designed by a registered design professional; heavy-duty 300 psf where material is stored

Pedestrian protection — BC 3307

  • Clear path minimum 5 feet, free of obstruction, accessible
  • Walking surface designed for not less than 150 psf
  • Mirrors at all blind corners; ramps at 1:12 max with 5-foot landings
  • Flagpersons for intermittent operations adjacent to public areas
  • Warning signs at vehicle entrances, hoisting areas and chute discharge
  • No illuminated business or advertising signage on any protective structure

Tenant protection plan — AC 28-120.1

  • No interruption of heating, water or electrical service to tenants
  • No change to existing paths of egress; egress kept clear at all times
  • Dust control per OSHA 3902-07R respirable crystalline silica Table 1
