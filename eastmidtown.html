Project Overview

Six buildings, one envelope program

Exterior envelope restoration across all six buildings of East Midtown Plaza under FISP (Local Law 11) Cycle 9 — concrete spall and crack repair, eyebrow-slab and expansion-joint waterproofing, sealant replacement, owner-supplied brick replacement and pointing, A/C cover and metal sill replacement, and full-height façade inspections. Executed two phases at a time, building by building.

Capabilities

What Pyramid self-performs

Façade Restoration

Local Law 11 / FISP inspections, decorative stone replacement & patching, waterproofing, investigation & probing, crack repair regardless of material.

Structural Steel Repairs

Beam repairs & modification, steel reinforcement & re-framing, column repairs, spandrel & dunnage work, scrape/prime/paint, waterproofing of members.

Roofing Replacement & Rehab

Full replacement & recovery, base-flashing & patching, green roof & solar, dunnage, water-tower & bulkhead rehab. Approved installer: Siplast, Sika, Kemper, Soprema, Firestone, GAF, TQ3.

Landmark Preservation

Cornice & water-table repair, decorative stone patching/re-anchoring, GFRC, historic window restoration, sheet-metal fabrication — LPC-compliant in-kind replication.

Terra-Cotta & Masonry

Unit replacement, repointing, corner reconstruction, patch/crack/glaze repair, marble Dutchman & pin repair, ornamental terra-cotta removal & re-set.

Scaffolding, Rigging & Sheds

Suspended & pipe scaffold design/install, sidewalk bridging (incl. Urban Umbrella), special rigger, site-safety plans & managers for buildings over 16 stories.

Sidewalk & Vault

Curb patching through full vault repair and sidewalk replacement, all agency permitting managed end-to-end.

Balcony & Terrace

Slab restoration & patching, pedestrian traffic coatings, railing code-compliance & re-coating, drainage and ponding solutions.

Garage / Parking Structures

Topside & underside concrete repair, traffic coatings, steel & Q-decking repair, expansion joints, post-tension repair, striping.
