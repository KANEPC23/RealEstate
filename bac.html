  • No slot pressure, no holding, no long taxi to the FBO

  • Same Fairfield County ground destinations, shorter drive from I-84

Home › About BAC

On this field since 1980.

Business Aircraft Center is a fixed base operation on the north side of Danbury Municipal Airport, occupying five acres and 31,500 square feet of hangar and office space — the largest FBO on the field.

A family business that happens to run a ramp.

Tango Yankee, LLC is the parent company of Business Aircraft Center and Danbury Aviation, a self- and full-service aircraft and pilot planning center at Danbury Municipal that includes aircraft management, hangar storage, tie-downs, and detailing.

Tango Yankee is owned and operated by Santo and Lynda Silvestro of New Canaan, who also own Hoyt Livery, New Canaan Auto Body, New Canaan Auto Repair, and the Veggie Barn Deli. They're active in the community and members of the New Canaan Chamber of Commerce — which is why your car is usually already waiting.
