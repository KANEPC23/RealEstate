Thank you for the opportunity to submit this proposal for the renovation of the fixed seating and platform systems in Lamport Auditorium. Scope of Work

1. Seating Removal & Floor Preparation

  • Surgically remove and dispose of the existing fixed auditorium seating, including all seat anchors and fasteners; salvage or preservation of the historic cast-iron standards (including Star of David detailing) coordinated with the Owner.
  • Patch, repair, smooth, and polish all floor penetrations remaining from the removed anchors and fasteners.
  • Two-stage floor preparation — clean, degrease, and profile the concrete floor and platform to receive the coating system.
  • Protect adjacent historic finishes (leaded art-glass windows, wall paneling, and stage) throughout the work.

2. Epoxy Flooring & Platform

  • Apply Benjamin Moore High Performance HP4400 Waterborne Epoxy floor system in a two-stage application — durable, two-component, chemical- and abrasion-resistant gloss. The system is allowed full manufacturer cure plus additional cure time to ensure it is completely set before seating installation and use. (Color by Owner.)
  • Clean floor diffuser / HVAC vents within the work area.
  • Furnish and install new commercial-grade aisle carpet runners in light blue, color-matched to the existing runners (length per approved seating plan / field measure).

3. Seating Layout & Anchoring

  • Prepare the seating floor plan and footprint, and set out the seating using a patented laser-guided layout system for precise, consistent alignment.
  • Secure the new seating with a modern floor-anchoring system engineered to accommodate the additional weight of the new seats.
  • Provide accessible seating — 11 wheelchair spaces and 11 companion seats, dispersed and integrated into the seating plan, plus designated aisle seats — delineated on the approved seating plans in accordance with the 2010 ADA Standards and NYC Building Code Chapter 11.

4. New Seating — Furnish & Install

  • Furnish, deliver, and install 1,129 new Convention-model fixed theatre seats — upgraded integral lumbar-support backs, engraved seat number / row-letter plates, and molded armrests (solid-wood armrest upgrade available; see Owner Options).
  • Optional integrated seat and aisle lighting, including associated electrical work to the seats and floor system (see Owner Options).
  • Seat construction: maintenance-free gravity-lift self-rising mechanism (no springs to fail over time); 4" cold-cured molded foam seat over five no-sag serpentine springs with woven nylon chafe barrier; 2" cold-molded lumbar-support foam back; 14-gauge steel standards; 3/4-length upholstered aisle-end panels; Interweave by Tahoe fabric (100% polyolefin, Cal 117, 250,000+ double-rub abrasion, 10-year warranty).
  • Provide a seat sample for Owner approval and field-verify the final seat count prior to production.
  • Total seat count: 1,129 seats (final count subject to code-required aisle widths and row spacing).

Accessibility & Seating Plan

This project replaces all fixed seating and therefore requires an accessibility-compliant seating plan. Preparation and approval of the accessible seating plan — providing the required wheelchair spaces, companion seats, and designated aisle seats and delineating them for the NYC Department of Buildings — is by others (the Owner's registered design professional) and is not included in this proposal. Because the seating is a primary-function area, altering it may also trigger accessible path-of-travel obligations (entrance and route), capped at 20% of the project cost; such work is by others. Sentinel will install the new seating in accordance with the approved accessible seating plan.
